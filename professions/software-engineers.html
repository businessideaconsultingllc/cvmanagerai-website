The Technical Dilemma

As a software engineer, your resume is inherently complex. You have to fit specialized languages, deep-stack frameworks, complex system design achievements, and CI/CD pipelines into a readable one-page document. Standard templates break under this density, and writing bullet points that balance technical depth with business impact is incredibly difficult.
