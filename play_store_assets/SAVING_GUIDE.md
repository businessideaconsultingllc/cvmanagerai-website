# How to Save Your Play Store Assets (Using Paint)

Follow this guide to get the exact resolutions Google Play requires.

## 1. Prepare Your Browser
1.  Open the **Asset Generator** page I made for you.
2.  Press **`Ctrl + 0`** (Zero) to reset your zoom to 100%. This ensures the images are the correct size on your screen.
3.  Press **`F11`** to go Full Screen (optional, helps fit everything).

---

## 2. Feature Graphic (Important!)
**Required Resolution:** `1024 x 500` pixels.

1.  **Capture:**
    *   Press `Win + Shift + S` (Snipping Tool).
    *   Select the **Feature Graphic** (the rectangle with "Build. Tailor. Succeed."). Try to secure the edges exactly.
2.  **Paste & Verify in Paint:**
    *   Open **Paint**.
    *   Press `Ctrl + V` to paste.
    *   Look at the **Button Bar** at the bottom of the Paint window. Ideally, it says `1024 x 500px`.
    *   **If it's different:**
        *   Click the **Resize** button (or `Ctrl + W`).
        *   Select **Pixels** (not Percentage).
        *   Uncheck "Maintain aspect ratio" (only if you need a tiny adjustment).
        *   Set Horizontal: **1024**, Vertical: **500**.
        *   Click OK.
3.  **Save:**
    *   File > Save As > **PNG picture**.
    *   Name it: `feature_graphic.png`

---

## 3. Phone Screenshots (x4)
**Recommended Resolution:** `1000 x 1000` pixels (Square) OR `1080 x 1920` (Portrait).
*Using the squares from my generator (`1000x1000`) is perfect for the Store.*

1.  **Capture:**
    *   Scroll to the first phone mockup (e.g., "Smart Dashboard").
    *   Press `Win + Shift + S`.
    *   Select the **entire squarish area** (the background color + phone).
2.  **Paste & Save:**
    *   Open Paint (Press `Ctrl + N` for a new file).
    *   Paste (`Ctrl + V`).
    *   **Verification:** Ensure it is roughly **1000 x 1000** pixels (or at least above 320px). It doesn't have to be exact like the Feature Graphic, just consistent.
    *   File > Save As > **PNG picture**.
    *   Name it: `screenshot_1_dashboard.png`.
3.  **Repeat** for the other 3 screenshots (`screenshot_2_builder.png`, etc.).

---

## 4. Upload to Google Play Console
1.  Go to **Store Presence > Main Store Listing**.
2.  **Phone Screenshots:** Drag and drop your 4 `screenshot_*.png` files.
3.  **Feature Graphic:** Upload your `feature_graphic.png`.
4.  **Save**.

You are done! 🚀
