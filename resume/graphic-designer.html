4 Tips for a Designer Resume That Passes ATS

1. Link to your portfolio prominently

ATS might not see your images, but it will see your URL. Make sure your Portfolio link is in the header in plain text.

2. Use standard font names

Creative fonts can break ATS parsers. Stick to web-standard fonts for the document itself; let your portfolio show your typography skills.

3. Describe the "Why" behind your designs

Instead of "Made logos," write "Developed 3 logo concepts that aligned with target demographic, increasing user trust by 20%."

4. Avoid images of text

Never save your resume as a flat JPG or PNG. The ATS cannot read the text inside an image. Use our AI to export a search-friendly PDF.
