4 Tips to Pass ATS as a Product Manager

1. Mention your product framework

Include "OKRs", "Jobs-to-Be-Done", or "RICE prioritization" — these are specific signals that screeners and ATS look for.

2. Show scope and scale

Mention DAU, MAU, revenue, or team size. "Managed product serving 1M users" is far more compelling than "managed product."

3. Include technical fluency signals

List SQL, APIs, or analytics tools. Modern PMs are expected to be data-fluent; showing this wins over ATS and interviewers.

4. Quantify your GTM launches

For each shipped product, include a metric: adoption rate, revenue contribution, or NPS improvement.
