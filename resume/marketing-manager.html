Tips for an ATS-Optimized Marketing Resume

1. Quantify campaign results

Use exact numbers: revenue generated, ROAS, subscriber count, or conversion rate improvements. ATS loves numbers.

2. Include platform names

Name every tool — "Google Ads", "HubSpot", "Mailchimp" — as standalone keywords. Don't just say "digital marketing tools."

3. Show budget ownership

Mention the budget you managed. "Managed $500K annual ad budget" signals seniority and responsibility to ATS and humans.

4. Highlight cross-functional leadership

Marketing managers collaborate with sales, product, and design. Mention these partnerships to score higher on leadership criteria.
