5 Tips for an ATS-Ready Data Analyst Resume

1. Lead with your analytical tools

List SQL, Python, Tableau, and Power BI prominently in your Skills section — these are the first things ATS scans for.

2. Turn analysis into outcomes

"Analyzed sales data" is weak. "Identified $300K revenue leak via cohort analysis" is powerful and ATS-friendly.

3. Include business context

Mention the industry (e-commerce, fintech, healthcare) to help ATS match you to industry-specific roles.

4. Mention team collaboration

Data analysts work with engineering, product, and marketing. Show cross-functional collaboration in your bullets.

5. Add a portfolio or GitHub link

Include a link to your GitHub or portfolio site. It differentiates you from candidates with identical technical skills.
