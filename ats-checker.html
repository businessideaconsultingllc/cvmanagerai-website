Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ATS resume checker?

An ATS resume checker analyzes your resume text against standard ATS criteria: keyword density, formatting, section structure, and job-description match. It gives you a score from 0–100 and shows exactly what needs improving.

How does the ATS checker work?

This free tool uses a high-speed JavaScript engine to scan for key patterns and industry keywords. Our main AI app uses advanced Large Language Models (LLMs) to perform a much deeper, semantic analysis of your career history. Because the app uses AI to "read" your resume like a human recruiter, scores may vary slightly between the two versions.

What is a good ATS score?

A score of 75%+ is considered competitive. A score of 85%+ puts you in the top candidates. Below 60% means your resume is likely being automatically filtered out before a human sees it.

Is this ATS checker really free?

Yes, completely free. No account, no credit card. For a full AI-powered fix, use our free AI resume builder.

Why does my resume score low even though I'm qualified?

ATS systems don't evaluate qualifications — they match text patterns. The most common reasons for low scores are: missing keywords from the job description, non-standard formatting, and lack of measurable achievements. All fixable in minutes with AI.
