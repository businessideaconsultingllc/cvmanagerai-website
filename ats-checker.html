FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ATS resume checker? ▾

An ATS resume checker analyzes your resume against standard ATS criteria: keyword density, formatting, section structure, and job-description match. It gives you a score from 0–100 and shows exactly what needs improving.

How does the ATS checker work? ▾

This free tool uses a fast JavaScript engine to scan for key patterns and industry keywords. Our main AI app uses advanced LLMs for a much deeper semantic analysis — scores may vary slightly between both tools.

What is a good ATS score? ▾

A score of 75%+ is considered competitive. A score of 85%+ puts you in the top candidates. Below 60% means your resume is likely being automatically filtered out before a human sees it.

Is this ATS checker really free? ▾

Yes, completely free. No account, no credit card. For a full AI-powered fix, use our free AI resume builder.

Why does my resume score low even though I'm qualified? ▾

ATS systems don't evaluate qualifications — they match text patterns. Common reasons: missing keywords, non-standard formatting, lack of measurable achievements. All fixable in minutes with AI.
