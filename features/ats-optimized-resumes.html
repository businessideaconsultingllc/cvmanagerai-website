ATS Optimized AI Resumes

Most large employers now filter resumes with software before a human ever reads them. We help make sure yours gets through.

Why Qualified Applicants Get Filtered Out

You applied for 50 jobs this month, you match the requirements, and you heard nothing back. Often the reason isn't your experience — it's that a human never read the document. Most mid-size and large employers run applications through an Applicant Tracking System first, and a resume that the software can't parse cleanly, or that doesn't contain the terminology the posting used, can be ranked out before anyone sees it.

What Actually Breaks ATS Parsing

These are the specific, mechanical failures that cost people interviews:

Multi-column layouts. A parser reads left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Two columns often get merged into nonsense — "Experience: Google" beside "Skills: Python" becomes one garbled line, breaking both entries.
Text inside images, tables, or text boxes. If your name and contact details sit in a header graphic or a table cell, many systems simply won't extract them — and a candidate record with no email is a dead record.
Creative section headings. "My Journey" and "What I Bring" don't map to the fields the software expects. "Work Experience," "Education," and "Skills" do.
Inconsistent or missing dates. Parsers look for a recognizable date-range pattern to build your work history. Mixed formats across roles can produce gaps that aren't really there.

How CV Manager AI Handles This

Every CV the generator produces uses a single-column layout with standard section headings, consistent "Month Year – Month Year" dates, real selectable text rather than images, and simple hyphen bullets — so there's no column order to get wrong and nothing for a parser to choke on. Bullet points are written to lead with an action verb and include a measurable result, which is also what makes them read well to the human who sees them next.

For keyword matching, the tailoring tool takes a specific job description and adjusts which of your real skills and phrasing get foregrounded, so your CV speaks the same vocabulary as the posting. And the free ATS checker lets you scan any resume — ours or one you already have — for missing keywords and formatting problems before you submit it.

What We Don't Claim

No tool has access to the internal scoring logic of Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, or any other ATS — those systems are proprietary and configured differently by every employer that uses them. Anyone promising a guaranteed score against a named platform is guessing. What genuinely helps is universal: clean structure a parser can read, standard headings, and the actual terminology from the job description. That's what we build for, and it's a real advantage — just not a magic one.
