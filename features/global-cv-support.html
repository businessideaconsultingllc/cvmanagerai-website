Global AI CV Support

Take your career international. Professional resume generation in 11 languages with native RTL support.

The International Job Market

Applying for a role in Paris is a different exercise from applying to a startup in Silicon Valley or a corporate position in Dubai. Running an English CV through a translator usually produces text that is grammatically correct and still obviously foreign — professional writing carries conventions that don't survive word-for-word conversion, and a local recruiter notices immediately.

Generated in the Language, Not Translated Into It

When you pick a target language, the AI writes your CV in that language from the start rather than drafting in English and converting afterward. The difference shows up in the parts translation handles worst: professional idiom, the conventional phrasing for a summary section, and the verbs that sound natural in a work context rather than merely accurate. You get the same editing control as any other CV — review it, adjust anything, then download.

Right-to-Left Support for Arabic

Arabic is the case most tools get wrong. Right-to-left text needs more than a font swap — alignment, bullet direction, and the way mixed Arabic-and-Latin strings (an English company name inside an Arabic sentence, for instance) are laid out all have to be handled correctly, or the PDF renders as visually broken even when every word is right. Arabic CVs are generated with proper RTL layout and export to PDF without the reversed-punctuation and misaligned-bullet artifacts that usually give away a machine-translated document.

What Language Selection Does Not Change

Being clear about the boundary: choosing a language changes the language, not the document's structure. It won't add a photo for markets that expect one, insert the personal-details block a French or German CV conventionally includes, or switch to a country-specific layout. The output is one clean, ATS-friendly single-column format in whichever language you chose. For country-specific conventions, our CV by country guides cover what to add or remove, and you can use the "Additional Information" field to ask the AI for specific local phrasing.
