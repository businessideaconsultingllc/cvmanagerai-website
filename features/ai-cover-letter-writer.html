AI Cover Letter Writer

Write a highly personalized, compelling cover letter in 10 seconds without the anxiety.

The Forgotten Advantage

Most people dread writing cover letters — they feel repetitive, boastful, and easy to skip. But a well-crafted one is often your only real opportunity to explain a career gap, show genuine interest in the company, and demonstrate how you communicate before an interviewer ever talks to you. A CV lists what you did; a cover letter is where you get to say why it matters for this particular job.

What the Generator Needs From You

Three inputs, and the more specific the third one is, the better the letter:

Your CV. Select one you've already saved, upload a PDF or TXT, or paste the text. This is where the achievements in your letter come from — it won't invent accomplishments that aren't in your history.
The company name and job title. Used in the opening and to make the letter specific rather than a form letter with a name dropped in.
The job description. Optional but strongly recommended — this is what lets the AI pick which of your achievements to lead with instead of guessing.

Why It Writes Short Letters on Purpose

The generator produces three paragraphs, around 220-320 words, and that constraint is deliberate. The most common failure in a cover letter isn't a bad sentence — it's length. A letter that walks through every role on your CV duplicates the document already attached to it and stops being read halfway down. Three paragraphs forces the useful shape: why this role and company, your single strongest relevant achievement with a real number attached, then one more strength and a brief close. Hiring managers read a lot of these, and the ones that get read to the end are short.

Before You Send It

Read it once, properly. The AI works from what your CV says, so if a bullet point there overstates something, the letter will repeat it more confidently. Check that the company name is right everywhere, that any claim about why you want to work there is something you'd actually say out loud in an interview, and that it sounds like you. A cover letter is the one document in your application that's explicitly about your voice — an unedited AI draft that reads as generic defeats the purpose of sending one at all. Generating a cover letter costs 1 credit, and you can download the finished letter as a PDF with your name and contact details in the letterhead.
