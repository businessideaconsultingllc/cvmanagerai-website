Stop staring at a blank page. Let AI write your professional resume from scratch in under 60 seconds.

The Death of the Blank Page

The hardest part of writing a resume is starting. Staring at a blank document while trying to remember every achievement from the last five years is exhausting — and the result is usually a duty list ("responsible for managing the team") rather than the achievement-and-metric format hiring managers actually scan for. Traditional resume builders hand you an empty template and leave the writing, which is the genuinely hard part, entirely to you.

What You Actually Enter

The generator asks for three things, and only the first two are required:

1. Your personal details. Name, email, phone, address, and optionally LinkedIn and GitHub. These carry straight through to the finished document — the AI doesn't invent or alter them.
2. Your target job title. This is what shapes the whole draft — the summary, which skills get prioritized, and the vocabulary used in your bullet points.
3. Additional information (optional). This field is a free-text instruction to the AI. Leave it blank and you get a solid general CV for that role. Use it — "I have 6 years at a fintech startup, led a team of 4, and I want the summary to emphasize leadership" — and the AI works those specifics into the right sections.

What Comes Out

A complete, editable draft with a professional summary, work experience in reverse-chronological order with achievement-focused bullet points, a categorized skills section, education, certifications, and languages. Every bullet is written to start with an action verb and include a measurable result where one makes sense, because that's the structure both ATS keyword matching and human reviewers respond to.

Nothing is locked. The draft opens in a full editor where you can rewrite any section, add or delete roles, fix anything the AI got wrong, and then download as a PDF. Treat the AI output as a strong first draft that saves you the blank-page problem — not as a finished document you're stuck with.

Honest Limitations

The AI only knows what you tell it. If you enter a job title and nothing else, it produces a competent but generic CV built around typical responsibilities for that role — you'll need to edit in your real companies, dates, and numbers. It also can't verify anything: if a generated bullet point claims a result you didn't achieve, that's on you to correct before sending it anywhere. The output uses one clean, single-column layout designed for ATS parsing rather than a gallery of visual template choices.

Key Benefits & Features

  • Zero Writer's Block: A complete first draft instead of an empty page.
  • Achievement Formatting Built In: Action verbs and measurable results by default, not duty lists.
  • Fully Editable: Change anything before you download — the AI drafts, you decide.
  • 11 Languages: Generated natively in your target language, not machine-translated after the fact.
