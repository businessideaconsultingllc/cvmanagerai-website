CV Manager AI vs Zety: What You Need to Know

Zety is a well-established resume builder used by millions worldwide. It offers professional templates, a guided step-by-step builder, and pre-written text suggestions. However, it operates on a subscription model that limits access to downloads and advanced features behind a paywall. CV Manager AI is newer and focused on deep AI integration — including job-description tailoring and contextual AI writing — with a more accessible pricing structure.

This comparison is based on publicly available information and hands-on testing in early 2026. We acknowledge Zety's strengths honestly.

⚖️ Our Honest Take

Zety is a genuinely good product. Its templates are polished, the builder experience is guided and beginner-friendly, and it has a large user base with proven results. Its biggest friction point is the paywall around PDF downloads — you invest time building your resume and then hit a payment gate. CV Manager AI removes that barrier entirely. Where we genuinely outperform Zety is in live AI tailoring to job descriptions and keyword optimization — Zety uses pre-written text banks while we generate contextually from your input. However, Zety's template quality and LinkedIn import are areas where they still have an edge.

Bottom Line

If you want a beautifully guided experience and don't mind the subscription cost, Zety is a solid choice. If you want AI-powered tailoring, free downloads, and real-time ATS optimization without a subscription, CV Manager AI is the better fit.
