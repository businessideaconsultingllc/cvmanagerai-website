Navigating the UK Market

In the UK, a 'CV' is standard rather than a 'resume.' Personal details like photos or birth dates are generally avoided, and it is common for senior professionals to extend to two pages. The emphasis is on a strong personal profile and a clear, evidence-based career history formatted in British English spelling.

How CV Manager AI Accelerates Your Success
