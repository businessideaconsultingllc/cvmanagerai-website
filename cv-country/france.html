What Is the Standard French CV Format?

The French CV (curriculum vitae) follows specific conventions that differ from the Anglo-American resume. French employers value a structured, chronological document that includes personal details, professional experience, and education in a specific order. Our AI CV builder automatically formats your CV to meet French market expectations.

Key Differences: French CV vs. English Resume

📸 Photo Included

Unlike in the US/UK, it is standard and expected to include a professional photo on a French CV.

👤 Personal Details

Include date of birth, nationality, and marital status if desired — this is standard in France despite being uncommon elsewhere.

📝 1–2 Pages Maximum

French CVs should be concise: 1 page for new graduates, 2 pages maximum for senior professionals.

🇫🇷 Written in French

All applications for French companies should be submitted in French unless the job explicitly requires English.

Standard French CV Structure

  • 1️⃣ En-tête (Header) — Name, photo, contact details
  • 2️⃣ Profil / Accroche — 3-line professional summary (optional but recommended)
  • 3️⃣ Expériences Professionnelles — Reverse chronological work experience
  • 4️⃣ Formation — Education (Grandes Écoles are highly valued in France)
  • 5️⃣ Compétences — Skills: languages, tools, certifications
  • 6️⃣ Centres d'Intérêt — Hobbies (commonly included in France)

Tips for a Successful Candidature in France

Always include a lettre de motivation

French employers almost universally expect a cover letter (lettre de motivation) alongside your CV. Our AI generates both simultaneously.

Highlight your Grande École education

If you attended Sciences Po, HEC, or a top engineering school, put this prominently. French hiring culture places enormous weight on educational prestige.

Use formal language

French professional communication is formal. Avoid casual phrases. Our AI defaults to formal register for French-language CVs.

Tailor for French ATS systems

Large French companies also use ATS. Make sure your CV includes the exact keywords from the offre d'emploi (job posting).
